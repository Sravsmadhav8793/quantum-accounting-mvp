# Quantum Accounting Play listing draft

- Package: `com.sravantix.quantum`
- Category: Finance
- Price: Free
- Default language: English (United States)

**Short description:** Keep transactions, invoices, expenses and reports in one private workspace.

**Full description:**

Quantum Accounting gives authorized users a place to review transactions, import bank statements, manage invoices and expenses, and view financial reports. Sign in with your approved account using a password, Google, or a registered device passkey. An existing account is required; the app does not offer public registration. Internet access is required.

**Release notes:** Initial Android release with private account sign-in and device passkeys.

**Privacy policy:** https://quantum.sravantix.com/privacy.html

**Account deletion requests:** https://quantum.sravantix.com/delete-account.html

Review the wording, Data safety responses, and app access instructions against the deployed behavior before submitting to Google Play.
